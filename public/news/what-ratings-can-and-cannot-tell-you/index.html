Ratings guide

What a movie rating can—and cannot—tell you

September 4, 20265 min readBy TShow Editorial

Ratings help narrow a large catalog, but the vote count, audience and your own taste matter just as much.

A rating is a compressed opinion from a particular audience. Compare it with the number of votes: a stable score from many viewers usually means something different from an early score based on a small group.

Genre matters too. Horror, experimental cinema and children's titles can have devoted audiences while looking weaker in broad averages.

Use ratings to shortlist, then read the overview, check the cast and watch an official trailer. TShow labels the source of a score so viewers do not mistake a TMDB community rating for an IMDb rating.
