Build a better weekend watchlist

September 4, 20263 min readBy TShow Editorial

A simple three-part mix: one crowd favourite, one short series and one wildcard chosen outside your usual genres.

A useful watchlist is short enough to finish. Begin with one popular film you already wanted to see, add a limited or short-form series, then choose one highly rated title from a genre you rarely browse.

Save those three titles to My list. TShow stores the list in the current browser, so there is no account to manage and no viewing profile shared with an advertising network.

If the first choice does not fit the mood, move to the wildcard instead of spending the whole evening scrolling.
