Release desk

A calmer way to follow new releases

September 4, 20263 min readBy TShow Editorial

Use release dates as a planning tool, not a promise: schedules, regions and episode dates often move.

TShow's catalog highlights latest films and series airing today from connected metadata providers. The Calendar view can also group saved series when their next date is available.

Release information is best understood as a signal. A theatrical date may differ from a streaming date, and an episode can arrive at different local times in different countries.

For an important premiere, verify the date with the studio, broadcaster or streaming service in your region.

About this guide

TShow publishes original discovery guides based on public metadata. Availability and release dates can change, so viewers should confirm details with the official provider.
