Viewing guide

How to find where a movie is streaming

September 4, 20264 min readBy TShow Editorial

A quick, privacy-friendly way to compare legitimate viewing providers without opening a dozen tabs.

Start with the title page in TShow and open Watch options. When TMDB provider data is available, TShow groups services by subscription, rent, buy and free-with-ads options for your selected region.

Provider availability changes by country and over time. Treat every listing as a discovery shortcut and confirm the final price, language and availability on the provider's official page before paying.

Trailers and provider links open from the viewer's device. User-installed add-ons remain private to that browser and should only be used with media the viewer is authorized to access.

About this guide

TShow publishes original discovery guides based on public metadata. Availability and release dates can change, so viewers should confirm details with the official provider.
