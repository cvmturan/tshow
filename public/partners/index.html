Useful partnerships, clear boundaries

TShow welcomes lawful film, television and technology partnerships that help viewers discover what to watch.

What we offer

Clearly labelled sponsorships on original editorial pages, curated collection partnerships and future privacy-respecting display placements.

Where ads never appear

Advertising is kept away from the video player, personal history, My list and private user-installed add-on screens. Sponsors cannot influence ratings or disguise promotions as independent recommendations.

Disclosure

Sponsored and affiliate content is labelled. An affiliate link may earn TShow a commission without changing the viewer's price. TShow does not accept partnerships that promote unauthorized access to copyrighted media.
