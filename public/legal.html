Terms of Use

Service role

Cvm Turan is a personal, non-commercial media interface. It provides search, metadata, trailers, a local-file player, and a way for a visitor to connect an independent Stremio-compatible add-on. Cvm Turan does not sell access to films or series and does not grant any licence to third-party content.

Independent add-ons

User-added add-ons are selected by the visitor, stored in that browser, and are not published, recommended, endorsed, or controlled by Cvm Turan. Their operators are solely responsible for their services. Sources returned by user-added add-ons open directly in an external player, compatible source app, or provider page; Cvm Turan does not proxy, download, cache, or transcode their video.

Your responsibilities

You may use only content that you own, that is in the public domain, that is provided under a valid licence, or that you are otherwise legally authorized to access. You must not use Cvm Turan to infringe copyright, bypass access controls, distribute unlawful material, attack networks, or violate an add-on provider’s terms.

Safety controls

Cvm Turan may reject unsafe protocols, private-network destinations, malformed manifests, excessive requests, or providers associated with security abuse or substantiated infringement complaints. Compatibility with an add-on does not mean that its content is lawful.

External services and availability

External players, add-ons, metadata services, and linked websites have their own terms and privacy practices. The service is provided “as is” without a promise that every title, source, device, or add-on will work. To the extent permitted by applicable law, the operator is not responsible for independent services or a visitor’s unlawful use.

02

Privacy Policy

Information kept in your browser

Your installed add-on addresses, random browser identifier, watchlist, playback progress, and data-saver preference are stored using browser local storage. They stay on that browser until you remove them or clear the site’s data. A local video opened through the file picker is not uploaded.

Information processed by the server

The server receives a random browser identifier, add-on manifest addresses, requested title identifiers, search queries, and ordinary connection information needed to answer requests and prevent abuse. Add-on manifests and results may be held temporarily in memory or a short-lived cache. There is no user-account database and Cvm Turan does not sell personal information.

Hosting and third parties

Render and network providers may process IP addresses and request logs under their own policies. Metadata/image providers and YouTube may receive technical connection data when their resources are loaded. When you choose an external source, its provider and the selected app receive the request directly from your device.

Your controls

Remove an add-on from the Add-ons screen to stop using it. Clear this site’s browser data to erase the browser-stored identifier, add-on list, watchlist, and progress. Do not place personal information in add-on addresses. This service is not directed to children.

Copyright Policy

Cvm Turan respects intellectual-property rights. It does not bundle a torrent or piracy provider. Visitors and independent add-on operators must have all permissions required for the content they access or make available.

A rights holder may request that access to a specific provider or link be disabled. The initial report should identify the protected work, the exact add-on or URL at issue, the reporter’s authority, and a good-faith statement that the disputed use is not authorized. Cvm Turan may disable a provider or source while reviewing a credible complaint and may restrict repeated misuse.

Until a dedicated private legal mailbox is published, begin a report through the project issue tracker. Issues are public: do not publish signatures, addresses, phone numbers, identification documents, or other sensitive information. Ask the operator to establish a private channel for any additional information.
