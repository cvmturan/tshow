01

Terms of Use

Service role

TShow is an independent media discovery interface. It provides search, metadata, trailers, original editorial content, a local-file player, and a way for a visitor to connect an independent compatible add-on. TShow does not sell access to films or series and does not grant any licence to third-party content.

Independent add-ons

User-added add-ons are selected by the visitor, stored in that browser, and are not published, recommended, endorsed, or controlled by TShow. Their operators are solely responsible for their services. Compatible MP4, WebM, and HLS sources connect directly from the provider to the visitor’s browser; other sources may open in an external player, compatible source app, or provider page. TShow does not proxy, download, cache, or transcode video returned by user-added add-ons.

Your responsibilities

You may use only content that you own, that is in the public domain, that is provided under a valid licence, or that you are otherwise legally authorized to access. You must not use TShow to infringe copyright, bypass access controls, distribute unlawful material, attack networks, or violate an add-on provider’s terms.

Safety controls

TShow may reject unsafe protocols, private-network destinations, malformed manifests, excessive requests, or providers associated with security abuse or substantiated infringement complaints. Compatibility with an add-on does not mean that its content is lawful.

External services and availability

External players, add-ons, metadata services, and linked websites have their own terms and privacy practices. The service is provided “as is” without a promise that every title, source, device, or add-on will work. To the extent permitted by applicable law, the operator is not responsible for independent services or a visitor’s unlawful use.

02

Privacy Policy

Information kept in your browser

Your installed add-on addresses, random browser identifier, viewing region, watchlist, playback progress, and data-saver preference are stored using browser local storage. They stay on that browser until you remove them or clear the site’s data.

Information processed by the server

The server receives a random browser identifier, add-on manifest addresses, requested title identifiers, search queries, and ordinary connection information needed to answer requests and prevent abuse. Add-on manifests and results may be held temporarily in memory or a short-lived cache. There is no user-account database and TShow does not sell personal information.

Contact messages

If you use the contact form, TShow validates the fields and prepares a draft in your device’s email application. TShow does not send the draft through a third-party form relay. The message reaches TShow only after you review it and press Send in your email application.

Hosting and third parties

Render and network providers may process IP addresses and request logs under their own policies. Metadata/image providers and YouTube may receive technical connection data when their resources are loaded. When you choose an external source, its provider and the selected app receive the request directly from your device.

Advertising and affiliate links

TShow does not currently load behavioral advertising. If advertising or affiliate links are enabled later, sponsored content will be labelled, this policy will identify the relevant providers, and consent controls will be added where required. Ads will not be placed inside the player or private add-on screens.

Your controls

Remove an add-on from the Add-ons screen to stop using it. Clear this site’s browser data to erase the browser-stored identifier, add-on list, watchlist, and progress. Do not place personal information in add-on addresses. This service is not directed to children.

Copyright Policy

TShow respects intellectual-property rights. It does not bundle a torrent or piracy provider. Visitors and independent add-on operators must have all permissions required for the content they access or make available.

A rights holder may request that access to a specific provider or link be disabled. The initial report should identify the protected work, the exact add-on or URL at issue, the reporter’s authority, and a good-faith statement that the disputed use is not authorized. TShow may disable a provider or source while reviewing a credible complaint and may restrict repeated misuse.

Send a private report to copyright@showt.fun or use the TShow contact form and choose “Copyright complaint.” Do not include identity documents or other sensitive information unless the operator specifically requests them through a verified reply.
